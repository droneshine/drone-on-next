# DRONE ON design notes

## Direction

- THESIS: The interface is the pilot's ground station, not a game menu: telemetry first, the field always visible behind it. Refuses the category default of neon esports chrome and stacked cards.
- OWN-WORLD: Evergreen #002518 ground, Off White text, Light Green #B5F78A as live telemetry, Accent Green #26C257 only for the armed/go state. Nasalization (Audiowide where it is not installed) for every number and command word, Inter for everything a pilot reads. Hairline rules, tabular numerals, no boxes inside boxes.
- STORY: A pilot lands on the field, sees a real drone idling in front of them, picks FLY, TRAIN or BUILD and is in the air in two clicks.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: Full bleed live 3D field with the selected drone hovering; left rail with the wordmark and five giant Nasalization commands; bottom right the drone's live spec line.
- FORM: Ground control station; extension of the established DroneShine brand.
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
